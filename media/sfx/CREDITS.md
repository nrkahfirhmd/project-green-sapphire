# Sound credits

Most sounds here are synthesised by `tools/make_sfx.py` and are original to this project.

The recorded (foley) sounds and ambience loops are cut by `tools/make_foley.py` from Freesound clips released
under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): no attribution is required, but they are listed here for the record. The
uncut sources live in `media/sfx_src/` (kept out of git).
